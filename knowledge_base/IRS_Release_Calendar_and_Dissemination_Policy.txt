INSTITUTUL ROMAN DE STATISTICA

RELEASE CALENDAR AND DISSEMINATION POLICY

Edition 2025.1. Approved by the President of IRS on 30 October 2024.
Applicable to all releases from 1 January 2025.


1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

1.1 Purpose
This document sets out the principles under which the Institutul Roman de
Statistica (IRS) disseminates official statistics, and the release calendar
for its principal monthly, quarterly and annual statistical releases in 2025.

1.2 Principles
IRS disseminates official statistics in accordance with the principles of
professional independence, impartiality, objectivity and equal access. All
users receive the same statistics at the same time. No user, including
government bodies, receives statistics before their publication, except under
the embargo arrangements of Section 3.


2. RELEASE CALENDAR

2.1 Advance announcement
The release calendar for the following calendar year is published no later
than 31 October. It states the date of each release and the reference period
it covers. Changes to the calendar are announced at least five working days in
advance, together with the reason for the change.

2.2 Release time
Releases are published at 09:00 Romanian time (EET in winter, EEST in summer)
on the IRS website and the IRS open data portal simultaneously.

2.3 Principal releases and timeliness

  Release                                          Frequency   Published
  -----------------------------------------------  ---------   ----------------
  Consumer price index (national)                  monthly     t + 12 days
  Industrial production index                      monthly     t + 40 days
  Average net earnings                             monthly     t + 40 days
  Regional monthly indicators (REG_LUNAR)          monthly     t + 45 days
  Registered unemployment (county level)           monthly     t + 45 days
  Labour force survey, quarterly results           quarterly   t + 75 days
  GDP, flash estimate                              quarterly   t + 45 days
  GDP, detailed estimate                           quarterly   t + 65 days
  Regional statistical yearbook                    annual      November

  "t" denotes the end of the reference period; lags are indicative and the
  exact dates are those of the calendar. Where the date falls on a
  public holiday or weekend, the release takes place on the next working day.

3. EMBARGO

3.1 Pre-release access
Pre-release access is limited to accredited journalists under embargo, for at
most 60 minutes before the release time, in a controlled room without external
communication. No pre-release access is granted to government bodies.

3.2 Breaches
A breach of the embargo is published on the IRS website and leads to the
withdrawal of accreditation.


4. PROVISIONAL DATA AND REVISIONS

4.1 Provisional data
Data for the most recent reference month of the regional monthly indicators,
industrial production and earnings are provisional. They are flagged "p" in
the dissemination tables and are revised in the next monthly release.

4.2 Routine revisions
Routine revisions are made in the release following the one in which the data
were first published. For REG_LUNAR, the values of the previous month may be
revised; values for earlier months are revised only in the annual revision
published with the data for March of each year.

4.3 Major revisions
Changes of base year, methodology or classification are announced at least
three months in advance in a methodological note. The most recent major
revision of price indices took place with the release of the data for January
2022, when the index reference period of national price indices was changed
to 2021=100.

4.4 Corrections
Errors are corrected as soon as they are identified. A correction notice is
published at the top of the release page stating the series and periods
affected and the nature of the error.


5. DISSEMINATION FORMATS AND REUSE

5.1 Formats
Statistical releases are published as press releases in Romanian and English,
as tables in the IRS online statistical database, and as machine-readable files (CSV and
XLSX) on the open data portal. Machine-readable files use the decimal point;
press releases and printed bulletins use the decimal comma.

5.2 Reuse and citation
IRS statistics may be reused free of charge for any purpose, including
commercial purposes, provided that the source is acknowledged as "Source:
Institutul Roman de Statistica (IRS)", together with the name of the dataset
and the date of extraction. Users who modify or combine IRS data shall state
that they have done so and shall not present their results as IRS statistics.

5.3 Metadata
Every dataset is published with reference metadata and, for structured files,
a data dictionary. Users should consult the metadata before interpreting data.
Columns or codes not defined in the metadata of a dataset have no IRS
definition.


6. CONTACT

User support: IRS Statistical Information Service, open on working days from
08:00 to 16:00. Requests for data not included in the release calendar are
answered within 15 working days.
