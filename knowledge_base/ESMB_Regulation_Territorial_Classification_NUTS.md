# ESMB Regulation on the Territorial Classification of Statistical Units (NUTS)

European Statistical Methods Board. Consolidated text, version 1.3, incorporating the NUTS 2021 classification applicable from 1 January 2021.

## 1 Subject matter and scope

### 1.1 Subject matter

This Regulation establishes the common Nomenclature of Territorial Units for Statistics (NUTS) for the collection, compilation and dissemination of regional statistics by the Member Statistical Authorities (MSAs) participating in the European Statistical Methods Board.

### 1.2 Scope

The Regulation applies to all regional statistics transmitted to or disseminated under ESMB standards. Regional statistics for which a territorial breakdown is required shall use the NUTS codes in force for the reference period of the data.

## 2 Definitions

For the purposes of this Regulation:

(a) "territorial unit" means a geographical area with a defined boundary that is used for the production of statistics;

(b) "administrative unit" means a territorial unit for which an administrative authority has the power to take administrative or policy decisions, in accordance with the legal and institutional framework of the country;

(c) "non-administrative unit" means a territorial unit formed by aggregating smaller administrative units, created for statistical purposes to meet the population thresholds of Article 4;

(d) "local administrative unit (LAU)" means a subdivision of a NUTS 3 region, such as a municipality or commune, used for local statistics;

(e) "extra-regio territory" means parts of the economic territory that cannot be attached to a single region, such as embassies abroad, air space and territorial waters, and deposits outside territorial waters.

## 3 Structure and codes

### 3.1 Hierarchy

NUTS is a hierarchical classification with three levels. Each country is subdivided into NUTS 1 regions, each NUTS 1 region into NUTS 2 regions, and each NUTS 2 region into NUTS 3 regions. Every territorial unit belongs to exactly one unit of the next higher level.

### 3.2 Coding

The code of a territorial unit starts with the two-letter country code, followed by one character for each level: one character for NUTS 1, two for NUTS 2 and three for NUTS 3. For example, the code RO113 identifies a NUTS 3 region in NUTS 2 region RO11, which belongs to NUTS 1 region RO1 in Romania (RO). The country code for Greece is EL and for the United Kingdom UK. Extra-regio territories are coded with the letter Z at each level, for example ROZ, ROZZ and ROZZZ.

### 3.3 Codes and names

The code is the identifier of a region. Names are given in the national language and may be written with or without diacritics, or translated, without changing the identity of the region. Statistical files shall therefore be matched on codes. Where only names are available, they shall be matched against the official names and the known variants published by the MSA, and unmatched names shall be reported rather than guessed.

### 3.4 Aggregates that are not NUTS regions

Codes such as EU27_2020 (European Union of 27 countries, composition from 2020) and EA20 (euro area of 20 countries) denote country aggregates. They are not NUTS regions and shall not be treated as such.

## 4 Classification criteria

### 4.1 Use of existing administrative units

Existing administrative units shall be used for NUTS levels wherever they meet the population thresholds. Non-administrative units shall be created only where no administrative units of suitable size exist at a level.

### 4.2 Population thresholds

| Level | Minimum population | Maximum population |
|---|---|---|
| NUTS 1 | 3 000 000 | 7 000 000 |
| NUTS 2 | 800 000 | 3 000 000 |
| NUTS 3 | 150 000 | 800 000 |

The average population of the regions of a level in a country shall lie within these thresholds. Individual regions may deviate where geographical, socio-economic, historical, cultural or environmental circumstances so require.

## 5 Amendments

### 5.1 Frequency

The classification shall not be amended more frequently than every three years. Amendments shall apply from 1 January of the second year following their adoption.

### 5.2 Back data

When the classification is amended, MSAs shall transmit time series for the new regional breakdown for at least the five years preceding the date of application, so that regional series remain comparable over time.

### 5.3 Versions

Each version is identified by the year of application, for example NUTS 2016 or NUTS 2021. Datasets shall state the NUTS version used. Data compiled on different NUTS versions shall not be combined at the level of the regions whose boundaries or codes changed.

## 6 Romania

### 6.1 Structure of the Romanian classification

In Romania the NUTS 1 regions are four macroregions, which are non-administrative units. The NUTS 2 regions are the eight development regions (regiuni de dezvoltare), established by law as units for regional development policy without administrative authority of their own. The NUTS 3 regions are the 41 counties (judete) and the Municipality of Bucharest, which are administrative units. Below NUTS 3, the LAUs are municipalities, towns and communes.

### 6.5 Name variants

Romanian names are listed without diacritics. Common variants include Bistrita-Nasaud for Bistrița-Năsăud, Dimbovita for Dambovita, Vilcea for Valcea, and Bucharest or Municipiul Bucuresti for Bucuresti (RO321). The NUTS 2 region RO32 Bucuresti-Ilfov and the NUTS 3 region RO321 Bucuresti are different units: RO32 also includes Ilfov county.

### 6.6 Regional data for Romania

Monthly regional indicators for Romania are generally available at NUTS 3 level (counties), while sample survey results such as labour force statistics are available at NUTS 2 level. When county data are aggregated to a development region, all counties of the region listed in Section 6.4 shall be included. A NUTS 2 value shall not be compared with the value of a single county.

## 7 Entry into force

This consolidated text applies from 1 January 2021. The previous version, NUTS 2016, remains applicable to data for reference periods before that date.
